Your privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

Kutralam Ezhilagam is a family-run stay at No 46, Five Falls Main Road, Vairams Nagar, Courtallam 627802, Tamil Nadu. This page explains what we do with the details you give us on kutralamezhilagam.com.

What we collect

  • When you book: your name, WhatsApp number, the room, dates and number of guests, and the payment reference from Razorpay.
  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo, as shared by Google. We do not get your Google password, contacts, files or anything else in your account.
  • When you talk or chat with Shenba: what you tell her about your stay, so the team can follow up.

How we use it

  • To reserve your room and record your booking.
  • To send your booking voucher on WhatsApp and your payment receipt to your email.
  • To show "Welcome back" and the receipts of your earlier stays when you sign in again.
  • To contact you about your stay, for example check-in details or a balance due.

We do not sell your details, and we do not send you advertising emails.

Who else sees it

Our booking team and front desk. Payments are handled by Razorpay, who receive the details needed to take the payment. Google handles the sign-in. Nobody else.

Payments

Card, UPI and net-banking details are entered on Razorpay's secure checkout. We never see or keep your card number or UPI PIN.

On your phone

The website keeps your sign-in and a copy of your receipts in your browser's storage so they appear quickly next time. Signing out from My Bookings removes your sign-in from that phone.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept as long as the law requires for hotel registers and accounts. Ask us and we will remove anything we are not required to keep.
